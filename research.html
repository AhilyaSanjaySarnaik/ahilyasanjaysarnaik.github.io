Poster · AICS 2026Malware detection

Adaptive retraining for malware detection under concept drift

Does ADWIN-triggered retraining beat a fixed schedule for detecting malicious Chrome extensions as the ecosystem changes?

Finding

Adaptive timing did not consistently beat evenly spaced retraining at the same frequency.

Trade-off

During a major ecosystem shift it caught more malware, but at the cost of substantially more analyst alerts.

Takeaway

Retraining frequency, training data and alert cost matter as much as how clever the schedule is.
