🧠 MSc research · featured

MSc Cybersecurity · National College of Ireland · 2026

Adaptive Gated Aggregation in Federated Learning for Network Security

View researchCase study

Organisations can train a shared network intrusion detector without handing over their traffic, but the shared model degrades when each participant sees very different data, a problem known as statistical heterogeneity or client drift. In this work each client trains an undercomplete autoencoder on its own normal traffic, and raw packets never leave the client. My aggregation method gates each update by its cosine similarity to the consensus direction, so poorly aligned updates are filtered out before the global model is built.

🏢 clients→local autoencoder, normal traffic only→model updates→cosine-similarity gate→aggregate→🌐 global NIDS

IJFMR · Volume 7, Issue 3 · May 2025

Intelligent Violence Detection: An AI Driven Approach for Enhanced Surveillance System

Read paperPDFDOI

An AI-powered surveillance system that spots violent behaviour and weapons in real time. It combines human detection, violence recognition and weapon detection, using deep learning models including Faster R-CNN, MobileNetV2, YOLO and MTCNN. When a threat is found, it sends a timestamped alert with location through Telegram, and stores events in Firebase Cloud Firestore for later review.

📹 camera feed→human detection→violence recognition→weapon detection→🚨 Telegram alert+Firestore log

🦠 Malware analysis research

MSc Cybersecurity, Malware Analysis · National College of Ireland · 2026

Pykspa Trojan Behaviour Analysis

An investigation of how the Pykspa malware family behaves, spreads and survives, with a focus on its domain generation algorithm (DGA) and the command-and-control infrastructure built on it. The work combines threat intelligence and vendor research with network forensics in Wireshark, and finds that the DGA, self-propagation, persistence and C2 design together explain why the botnet is so hard to take down.

threat intel→vendor research→behaviour analysis→DGA + C2→🦈 Wireshark forensics→defensive implications
  • Domain generation algorithms
  • Command and control
  • Botnet architecture
  • Propagation
  • Persistence
  • Registry interaction
  • Network traffic
  • Social engineering
  • Anti-analysis
  • Botnet resilience
  • Takedown challenges

Method note: public Pykspa packet captures were scarce, so DGA-style traffic was studied using an Asprox PCAP as an analogue. The report treats this as an approximation, not a direct observation of Pykspa traffic.
